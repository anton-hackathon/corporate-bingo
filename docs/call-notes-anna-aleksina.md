## Ideas

### Engagement

- **Photo of the day.** Collect the top five shots daily and send them out, or post them in a
  separate channel, with extra points for the most liked. Anton is against a constantly blinking
  chat: people will simply mute it.
- **Publish results in the morning for the previous day** — with Costa Rica in the mix, a European
  evening ranking is still mid-afternoon there.
- **An advent calendar as the model.** One door a day, which is where the daily return visit
  comes from.
- **A daily reminder email** so people do not forget the game exists.
- **Feature flags.** Introduce some mechanics later rather than on day one: it keeps the excitement
  up and lets the game get harder once the basics have sunk in.
- **Card lifetime.** A revealed card lives about 15 hours, or a day, and then becomes unavailable,
  so nothing can be hoarded for the last day. Anna is against it unless people are warned upfront.
- **Surprise events.** For instance, cards left undone after the first week close for good. Anna
  believes any rule like this has to be announced in advance.

### Mechanics

- **Anna:** simplify the scoring — however many team members did a task, that is how many points
  the team gets. Drop difficulty levels: difficulty is subjective.
- **Anton:** keep difficulty (as stars). It is what makes the scores granular and saves the round
  from the Commit to Fit tie.
- **Flexible team size** instead of hard-coded trios: five teams of ten, or six of four, depending
  on how many people turn up.
- **Model the balance.** Run batches of scoring scenarios overnight and see what comes out.
- **Hard tasks nearer the center, easy ones at the edges** — Anton himself suspects this is
  over-engineering.

### Task content

- An ugly Christmas sweater; a festive avatar.
- A bow tie on a team call: a cheap, available prop, and you can even cut one out of paper.
  A three-piece suit is too much.
- Come to the office in pyjamas; a photo of yourself aged five; your favourite hobby.
- Show your cat. No cat? Draw one on paper and hold it up: resourcefulness deserves a point too.
- The principle: a task should never require a shopping trip or money.

### Design

- Bring Karina in so the boards are drawn properly.
- Cartoon styling: Tom and Jerry are out on copyright grounds, but original characters work.

### Prizes

- Commit to Fit gave AirPods to every member of the winning teams, and the competition was fierce.
- The budget this time is unknown, but the leaders should get something.

## Open questions

1. **Team scoring or individual?** How to reward working together without punishing a team for one
   inactive member. Anna: "inside the team we motivate each other, and that is how we win or not."
   Anton: losing because of one person does not feel right.
2. **Are difficulty levels needed at all.** Without them there is no granularity; with them there
   is the argument about subjectivity.
3. **Team size** — fixed trios, or configurable to the actual headcount.
4. **Office-only tasks.** Anton: a third of the tasks tied to an office adds variety. Anna: that
   puts anyone without an office in their city at a built-in disadvantage. Unresolved.
5. **When to collect consent** for using photos. Anna: early, as a separate step once someone has
   already committed. Anton: later, behind a feature flag around day seven, so it does not scare
   people off at sign-up.
6. **How long a revealed card lives** — 15 hours, a day, or indefinitely.
7. **Whether surprise rules get announced.** Anna: unquestionably yes, otherwise people feel
   cheated. Anton thinks the surprise is part of the fun.
8. **Unique tasks.** Does every team need a distinct set, or are repeats between teams fine.
9. **Video as a proof type.** Tempting, but the load is roughly ten times higher because of file size.
10. **Where to host it and how to secure it.** The site sits outside the perimeter but holds company
    material. Performance and security are the reason Misha is wanted.
11. **Whether to drop surnames** from profiles. A work email gives them away anyway.
12. **Prize budget** — whether there is one.
13. **Availability of the working group.** Karina is back from holiday and may bring someone from
    her team. Misha and Mayela are both unconfirmed.
14. **Point balance** — how much for what. The first version will be a rough draft by definition.
